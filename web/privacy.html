Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Krewe of Christmas LLC (“Krewe of Christmas,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit kreweofchristmas.com (the “Site”) or request a quote for our Christmas light installation services.

Information we collect

When you browse the Site. The Site does not ask you to create an account, and we do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

When you request a quote. Our “Get a Free Quote” buttons open a form hosted by our quoting provider, Flyra. Through that form you may give us your name, email address, phone number, property address, and details about the lighting you want.

When you contact us directly. If you email or call us, we receive whatever information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, and your message.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and questions
  • To design, schedule, install, maintain, and take down your lighting
  • To send estimates and invoices and to keep records of our services
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as Flyra (quote forms) and our email provider, and only as needed to provide those services
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or the public
  • A successor business, if Krewe of Christmas LLC is ever sold or merged

Third-party services on the Site

  • Hosting. The Site is hosted on Google Firebase. Like most web hosts, it may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, to deliver and secure the Site.
  • Fonts. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded.
  • Quote form. Information you enter in the quote form is collected by Flyra and handled under its own privacy policy as well as this one.
  • Links. The Site links to our Instagram and Google Business pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

How long we keep information

We keep customer information for as long as we need it to provide our services, including seasonal installations and takedowns, and to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. After that, we delete it or keep it only in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing kreweofchristmas@gmail.com. We may need to keep some information when the law requires it, such as records of completed jobs and payments.

Children’s privacy

Our services are meant for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
